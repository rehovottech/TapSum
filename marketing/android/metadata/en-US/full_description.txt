Remember. Count. Tap!

TapSum is a bright, friendly mental math game. Numbers appear one at a time: add them up in your head, then tap the buttons to build the running total. Get it right and the next number arrives. Simple to learn, and great practice for adding, taking away and place value.

★ HOW TO PLAY
• A number appears: green means add, red means take away
• Keep the running total in your head
• Tap +1, +10, +100 and +1000 to build the answer, then press DONE
• Beat the timer before it runs out. You have three hearts

★ LEARNING THROUGH PLAY
• Colour-coded buttons for ones, tens and hundreds make place value easy to see
• Numbers grow as you go, from single digits to three-digit sums
• Missed one? The game shows the right total so you can carry on from it
• A guided first game teaches the rules step by step

★ LEVELS FOR EVERY AGE
• Little Learner (ages 5-8): small numbers and more time
• Explorer (ages 9-12): adding and taking away
• Brain Trainer (teens & adults): bigger numbers, faster rounds
Change who's playing any time. Each level keeps its own best score.

★ FUN TO COME BACK TO
• Streaks with confetti every five in a row
• Summy the mascot cheers you on
• Personal bests and a leaderboard for each level
• Short rounds, perfect for a few minutes of practice

★ FAMILY FRIENDLY
• Free to play
• Optional short video ad to get an extra heart, and an occasional ad between games
• No chat and no personal information asked for

TapSum is made by a tiny indie team. If you enjoy it, a rating means the world to us!
