## 1. Google Flow music prompts

### Reel 1 – "Remember. Count. Tap!" (21.4s, bright / sunny / kid-friendly)

**Prompt (paste this):**
```
Upbeat, sunny, playful mobile-game background music, 21 seconds, 112 BPM, C major.
Instruments: bouncy pizzicato strings, marimba and xylophone melody, soft ukulele strums,
light finger snaps, gentle kick and shaker, warm sub bass, tiny glockenspiel sparkles.
Feel: friendly, curious, "brain-game" fun for ages 5 to grown-ups. Clean, no vocals, no lyrics.

Structure:
0:00-2:40  Cheeky pizzicato hook with a curious rising phrase, minimal drums (a question being asked).
2:40-11:50 Steady groove: marimba pattern, soft kick and snaps, a slightly tense, focused "counting" loop.
11:50-16:50 Lift: add ukulele and clap, glockenspiel run up, bright celebratory feel.
16:50-21:20 Triumphant happy finale with a short stinger, then a clean ending on the tonic chord. No long fade.

Mix: leave space in the 1-5 kHz range for UI sound effects, no harsh highs, no heavy reverb. Loop-friendly.
Negative: no vocals, no EDM drops, no dark or cinematic tones, no distorted guitars, no sirens.
```

### Reel 2 – "Beat the Clock" (17.4s, high-energy / competitive)

**Prompt (paste this):**
```
High-energy, playful-competitive mobile game music, 17 seconds, 128 BPM, A minor shifting to C major.
Instruments: punchy synth plucks, chiptune-flavoured arpeggios, tight electronic drums with claps,
ticking clock-style hi-hats, fat round bass, sparkly synth risers. Colourful, fun, a bit cheeky.
No vocals, no lyrics.

Structure:
0:00-2:25  Fast riser with ticking hi-hats and a cut-through synth stab (urgency, "numbers flash!").
2:25-10:00 Driving beat, bouncy arpeggio, building tension like a countdown, drums tighten as the timer runs out.
10:00-12:40 Release: a one-beat silence, then a big "Boom!" hit into major-key payoff with sparkle arps.
12:40-17:20 Confident victory groove with a leaderboard-climb melody, ending on a short punchy button.

Mix: modern and clean, bass tight, nothing masking the 1-5 kHz UI sounds. Loop-friendly.
Negative: no vocals, no dubstep wobble, no dark horror tones, no harsh distortion.
```

### Short versions (if Flow limits prompt length)

- **Reel 1:** `Sunny playful mobile-game music, 112 BPM, marimba, pizzicato strings, ukulele, finger snaps, glockenspiel sparkles, curious intro, groove, happy celebratory ending, no vocals, 21 seconds.`
- **Reel 2:** `High-energy playful countdown game music, 128 BPM, synth plucks, chiptune arpeggios, ticking hi-hats, punchy drums, big boom hit then victory payoff, no vocals, 17 seconds.`

**Tips**
- Generate 3 to 4 variations per reel, then trim to length (hard stop on the tonic chord).
- Mix music at about -18 dB under the SFX (SFX peaks about -6 dB).
- Beats of note: Reel 1 confetti at ~10.4s and ~12.0s, end card at 16.7s. Reel 2 "Boom!" at ~9.1s, end card at 12.6s. Ask Flow for a lift or hit at those marks, or cut the track to them.
