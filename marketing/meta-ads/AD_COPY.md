# TapSum: Meta ad copy (Facebook and Instagram)

Use these with the images in this folder. Each concept has four sizes:

| File suffix | Use for |
|---|---|
| `feed-square_1080x1080` | Facebook and Instagram feed (works everywhere) |
| `feed-portrait_1080x1350` | Instagram and Facebook feed, takes more screen space |
| `story_1080x1920` | Stories and Reels (text is kept out of the top 14% and bottom 20%) |
| `link_1200x628` | Facebook right column, Audience Network, link ads |

Campaign objective: **App promotion**. The call-to-action button is **Install now**. Meta adds this button itself, so the images don't include one.

---

## Concept 1: `kids/` "Math practice kids love"

- **Primary text** (shows above the image):
  > Turn screen time into math time. 🧮 Numbers pop up one at a time; your child keeps the total in their head and taps to build it. Three levels, from age 5 up. Free to play.
- **Headline:** Math practice kids love
- **Description:** Free mental math game

## Rules to follow (Meta advertising standards plus the app's Families status)

- **Target parents, not children.** Meta requires an audience of at least 13, and many regions require 18. Suggested audience: ages 25–50, interests in parenting, homeschooling and education apps.
- **Don't promise results.** Avoid claims like "improves grades" or "boosts IQ by X". The copy above only describes what the game does.
- **Keep it truthful.** "Free to play" is accurate: the game is free, with optional ads and an optional donation.
- **Keep the images matching the real app.** All screens come from the current build, so update them if the UI changes. The images are regenerated with `source/render-promo.mjs`.
- **Ad copy that speaks to kids directly** ("Hey kids, download now!") isn't allowed. Speak to the parent instead.
