Concept only. Buttons demonstrate focus and feedback; no game, leaderboard, sound, or payment action is connected.

How to play

  • Each round shows a new plus or minus number.
  • Keep a running total of every number shown.
  • Tap +1, +10, +100 or +1000 to build up to that total.
  • Match it exactly to score and reach the next round.
  • Go over the total, or run out of time, and the game ends.

Leaderboard

Leaderboard data is connected in the game. This visual concept uses no invented player names or scores.
